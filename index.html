Years of Culinary Perfection

Since 1999

Crafting Royal Memories Through Authentic Food

Founded in the historic city of Jacobabad, Khan Jee Restaurant was created with a singular mission: to bring authentic Shinwari, Balochi Sajji, Peshawari BBQ, and Mughlai curries cooked without shortcuts, using pure animal fat, stone-ground garam masalas, and pristine cuts of mutton and chicken.

Every pot of Karahi is sizzled over high-flame iron woks right before your eyes. Our tandoor chefs masterfully slap garlic-flecked dough against volcanic clay ovens to bring you unmatched culinary joy.

✓ Pure Ingredients

Cold-pressed oils, pure butter, and unadulterated spices.

✓ Strict Hygiene

ISO certified food handling and spotless open kitchen.
